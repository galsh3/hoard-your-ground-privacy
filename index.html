AsgardZ · Hoard Your Ground

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Android package: com.hoardyourground.game

Your game progress stays on your device. The current game has no player accounts, active advertising, real-money checkout, or embedded analytics service. If you contact us or visit an online page, the separate processing described below applies.

1. Who we are and what this covers

AsgardZ, an independent developer based in Israel, publishes Hoard Your Ground. This policy covers the current Android game (version 0.12.0), its browser edition with the same features, this privacy page, and support communications. AsgardZ is responsible for personal information received through its support channel.

For privacy questions or requests, email asgardzasgardz@gmail.com.

2. Information used by the game

The game stores progress locally: coins, unlocked and selected dragons, upgrades, tricks, scores, achievements, Daily Siege results and reward dates, saved runs, and sound, accessibility, and control preferences. It uses these records to resume play, remember choices, and calculate progress and rewards. These records are not automatically sent to AsgardZ.

The current game does not request your name, email address, date of birth, contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier. It does not include an active analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK, cloud saves, online leaderboards, or player registration.

The browser edition uses local storage and caches game assets for offline play. It does not use advertising cookies. Export Progress creates a save file on your device; Import Progress reads the file you select locally. Exports are not automatically uploaded. If you share an export, its game data goes to whoever you choose.

3. Ads and purchases

Rewarded-ad and collector-dragon screens are present, but real ads and purchases are disabled in the current build. The game does not currently send data to advertising or payment providers. Admin practice mode simulates unlocks locally and makes no real purchase. Before activating services that change these practices, we will update this policy, identify the relevant providers and data uses, and provide notices and consent choices where required.

4. Support, hosting, and platform services

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use these to answer requests, troubleshoot issues, and handle privacy enquiries. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Email is handled through Gmail. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit this page, GitHub processes connection and request information such as IP address, browser information, and request time for hosting and security. This page contains no added analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party fonts.

Visiting the browser game also sends normal web requests to its hosting provider to deliver game files. These web-hosting requests are separate from local gameplay records. The installed Android game bundles its game files and does not need a hosted-page request to play.

Google Play and your device operating system separately handle app distribution, security, backups, and any diagnostics enabled by your platform settings. Google may make aggregated installation or stability reports available to developers. These platform services follow their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Sharing and international processing

AsgardZ does not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Support information is processed through our email provider and may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to address abuse, protect rights, or respond to your instructions. Providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States; AsgardZ operates from Israel. Provider policies describe their safeguards and processing locations.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local saves: remain until you clear them or the app/browser removes its storage. On Android, use Settings → Apps → Hoard Your Ground → Storage → Clear storage/data (wording varies by device). Uninstalling normally removes local app data. In a browser, clear site data for the game’s address. This resets progress.
  • Backups and exports: Android or device backups may preserve or restore app data depending on your settings. Manage backups through your device or Google account and delete exported save files separately. AsgardZ cannot remotely access or delete these copies.
  • Support messages: are kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and any related follow-up, security, or legal obligations. Request deletion by email; any required exception will be explained. Hosting and platform records follow the relevant provider’s retention policies.

There is no game account to delete. Clearing local storage deletes the game’s local profile; it does not delete an email you previously sent us.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, a copy of information, or restriction of or objection to processing of personal information we hold. Contact asgardzasgardz@gmail.com. We may need reasonable verification of the request. You may also contact your local data protection authority. Local gameplay records remain under your control on your device.

8. Children and security

The game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the game. If a child has sent personal information to our support channel, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and any exported save files; local saves are not a secure vault for sensitive information.

9. Changes and contact

We will publish updates on this page and revise the effective date. Material changes to data practices will be accompanied by additional notice and consent where required.
