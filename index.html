AsgardZ · Hoard Your Ground

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Android package: com.hoardyourground.game

Your game progress stays on your device. The game has no player accounts or active real-money checkout. Android versions 0.13.0 and 0.14.0 are internal testing builds with optional Google AdMob test ads. Version 0.14.0 applies child-directed ad treatment to everyone. Earlier version 0.12.0 and the current browser edition do not serve ads.

1. Who we are and what this covers

AsgardZ, an independent developer based in Israel, publishes Hoard Your Ground. This policy covers Android versions 0.12.0, 0.13.0 and 0.14.0, the browser edition, this privacy page, and support communications. AsgardZ is responsible for personal information received through its support channel.

For privacy questions or requests, email asgardzasgardz@gmail.com.

2. Information used by the game

The game stores progress locally: coins, unlocked and selected dragons, upgrades, tricks, scores, achievements, Daily Siege results and reward dates, saved runs, and sound, accessibility, and control preferences. It uses these records to resume play, remember choices, and calculate progress and rewards. These game-save records are not automatically sent to AsgardZ or used as ad-targeting parameters.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, date of birth, contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone. It has no cloud-save service, online leaderboard, or player registration. Android 0.13.0 and 0.14.0 include Google’s advertising and consent SDKs; their identifier and diagnostic processing is explained below. No separate gameplay analytics service is installed.

The browser edition uses local storage and caches game assets for offline play. It does not use advertising cookies. Export Progress creates a save file on your device; Import Progress reads the file you select locally. Exports are not automatically uploaded. If you share an export, its game data goes to whoever you choose.

3. Optional rewarded ads and purchases

Android versions 0.13.0 and 0.14.0 use Google AdMob for ads you choose to watch to revive once in an endless run or unlock one of three ad-reward dragons. No ad is required to start or continue ordinary play. A reward is granted only after the ad SDK confirms it was earned. These internal testing builds use Google test ads; test-ad mode is not a promise of zero data processing. Version 0.12.0 and the current browser edition have no active ads.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK can process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), interactions with ads and the app, performance and diagnostic details, and identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Version 0.13.0 uses the SDK’s default age treatment, under which available identifiers can include the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Version 0.14.0 sets child-directed treatment before initializing ads, disables personalized advertising through that treatment, and requests ads with a maximum content rating of G. Google states that child-directed treatment prevents transmission of the Android advertising ID. We do not send your exported saves, scores, or support emails as ad-targeting inputs.

The Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests current consent information at app launch. Before requesting ads, the app checks whether UMP permits ad requests. Version 0.13.0 displays a consent form when required. Version 0.14.0 marks every player as under the age of consent, so UMP does not request consent to personalized ads. The game does not collect ages. In Settings, choose AD PRIVACY to check available privacy options. You can always choose not to watch rewarded ads; ordinary gameplay remains available.

Google describes its processing in its Privacy Policy, Advertising information, and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit. We have not added third-party mediation networks or server-side reward verification. Google may provide advertising performance reports to AsgardZ.

Real-money purchases remain disabled. Version 0.14.0 includes the RevenueCat purchase SDK and Google Play Billing support for future testing, but the game does not configure the purchase SDK or request purchases while the store is disabled. We will update this policy before enabling purchases and the associated purchase-verification data processing. Admin practice mode simulates unlocks locally without making purchases or showing ads.

4. Support, hosting, and platform services

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use these to answer requests, troubleshoot issues, and handle privacy enquiries. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Email is handled through Gmail. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit this page, GitHub processes connection and request information such as IP address, browser information, and request time for hosting and security. This page contains no added analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party fonts.

Visiting the browser game also sends normal web requests to its hosting provider to deliver game files. These web-hosting requests are separate from local gameplay records. The installed Android game bundles its game files and does not need a hosted-page request to play.

Google Play and your device operating system separately handle app distribution, security, backups, and any diagnostics enabled by your platform settings. Google may make aggregated installation or stability reports available to developers. These platform services follow their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Sharing and international processing

AsgardZ does not sell your game saves or support messages. In the ad-enabled Android versions, the Google SDK shares ad-related information with Google for the purposes above. Version 0.14.0 applies child-directed treatment to all ad requests and does not request personalized advertising. Earlier internal version 0.13.0 may permit personalized advertising depending on consent and device settings; such processing may qualify as sharing for targeted advertising under some laws. Support information is processed through our email provider and may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to address abuse, protect rights, or respond to your instructions. Providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States; AsgardZ operates from Israel. Provider policies describe their safeguards and processing locations.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local saves: remain until you clear them or the app/browser removes its storage. On Android, use Settings → Apps → Hoard Your Ground → Storage → Clear storage/data (wording varies by device). Uninstalling normally removes local app data. In a browser, clear site data for the game’s address. This resets progress.
  • Backups and exports: Android or device backups may preserve or restore app data depending on your settings. Manage backups through your device or Google account and delete exported save files separately. AsgardZ cannot remotely access or delete these copies.
  • Support messages: are kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and any related follow-up, security, or legal obligations. Request deletion by email; any required exception will be explained. Hosting, advertising, consent, and platform records follow the relevant provider’s retention policies; consult Google’s linked policies and privacy controls for provider-held data.

There is no game account to delete. Clearing local storage deletes the game’s local profile; it does not delete an email you previously sent us.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, a copy of information, or restriction of or objection to processing of personal information we hold. Contact asgardzasgardz@gmail.com. We may need reasonable verification of the request. You may also contact your local data protection authority. Local gameplay records remain under your control on your device.

8. Children and security

Hoard Your Ground is intended for children aged 9 and older and adults. The game has no player registration and does not ask players for their age, name, email address, contacts, or precise location. Version 0.12.0 and the current browser edition do not serve ads. Versions 0.13.0 and 0.14.0 are internal testing builds, not the public family release. Version 0.14.0 applies the child-directed ad and under-age consent settings described above to every player. Parents and guardians can contact us about children's privacy. If a child has sent personal information to our support channel, a parent or guardian can request its deletion.

We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and any exported save files; local saves are not a secure vault for sensitive information.
